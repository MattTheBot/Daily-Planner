AI

Your key is stored in your own Firestore document and is only readable by your account. It never leaves your device except to call the provider directly.

OpenRouter (recommended): openrouter.ai → Keys → create key. Free tier: 50 requests/day, no card.
Z.ai and Groq: listed only for completeness — both refuse browser requests, so they will not work here.

Academic calendar

Set the date that was Day 1 of Week 1. Week and day numbers are calculated from this, and every AI call receives the current values.

Breaks & holidays

The AI is told about these so it can resolve "after half term" and similar phrasing.
